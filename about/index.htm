At NuIQ, our journey began with a simple yet profound idea rooted in solving billion dollar problems using technology. Recognizing the significance of this realm and its impact on overall well-being, we embarked on an adventure to bring advancing technology to the forefront.

Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs served as our guiding beacon. We saw an untapped potential to revolutionize the mental health space through innovative solutions.
